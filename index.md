# Ihsan Salat

Muslim prayer times app for **Uzbekistan**, **Kazakhstan** and Russian-speaking Muslims in **Russia**.
Flutter, offline-first, free with ads.

Coming to App Store and Google Play in early 2027 (soft launch in Kazakhstan), full launch for Ramadan 2027.
